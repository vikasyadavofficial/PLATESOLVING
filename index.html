Where is this photo pointing?

Upload a picture of the night sky. Plate Solver matches its stars against a catalogue and returns the exact coordinates, scale and rotation, usually within a minute.

Tips for a photo that solves
  • Use night mode or a long exposure. A few seconds makes the biggest difference, because the solver needs stars in the picture.
  • Keep the camera still. Use a tripod, or rest your phone on something steady and use the timer.
  • Zoom out and avoid bright lights. Use the widest lens, and keep street lamps and the bright Moon out of the frame.
  • Upload the original photo or FITS file. Screenshots and photos forwarded through messaging apps lose detail.
